# Research-Paper-
📚 Measuring Pedagogical Alignment in Online Learning Applications

This research project evaluates the educational effectiveness of online learning applications using Bloom's Taxonomy and Evidence-Based Learning (EBL) principles. The objective was to determine whether modern learning apps promote meaningful cognitive development or primarily rely on engagement techniques such as gamification, animations, and rewards.

The study involved designing a structured pedagogical evaluation framework, collecting survey responses through Google Forms, analyzing the gathered data, and interpreting the findings through visualizations and comparative analysis. The results provide insights into how well popular learning applications align with established educational theories and identify areas where they can be improved.
